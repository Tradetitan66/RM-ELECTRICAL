  equipment, or RM Electrical customer work, and must never be labelled as such.
- `about-rm.webp`, `lighting-project.webp`, `lighting-project-1.webp` and
  `lighting-project-2.webp` are **real RM Electrical project photographs** supplied
  by the client and are deliberately labelled as company work in the
  "ABOUT RM ELECTRICAL" and "LIGHTING PROJECTS" (carousel) sections.
- Source IDs are preserved for the stock image so it can be traced back to its Unsplash
  page for licence verification at any time.
- Replace images by saving new files over the same paths, or change the paths in
  `src/config/business.ts` (add an `images` block).
